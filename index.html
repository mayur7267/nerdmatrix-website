FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a website take?

Starter sites launch in about 7 days, Growth in 14 and Signature in 21–30. The clock starts once we have your logo, photos and content.

What do I need to give you?

Your logo, photos of your products or space, and your menu or service details. If you don't have good photos or copy yet, we can help with that as an add-on.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% when the site goes live. Prices exclude GST.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. Growth and Signature include easy content editing, so you can change menus, prices and photos without touching code. Or our Care Plan can handle updates for you.

Do I own the website?

Completely. The domain is registered in your name and you get full access to the code and every account we set up.
